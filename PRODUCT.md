# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Customers (guest, no login)** — order from a phone, standing in the shop or waiting for a pickup. They are in a task: browse, customize, check out fast, get a receipt number. Mobile-first, low friction, no account.

**Baristas / admin (login required)** — on a desktop or tablet behind the counter. They are in a workflow: see incoming orders in real time, update status, toggle item availability, glance at sales. Reliable, legible, fast.

## Product Purpose

A coffee shop online ordering website. Customers browse the menu by category (coffee, matcha, non-coffee, pastry, etc.), search, add items to a cart with per-item customization (size, sugar level, ice, extra shot, toppings), and check out with just a name + optional table number — receiving an order number/receipt. Baristas log in to manage menu items (CRUD + availability toggle), view incoming orders in real-time, advance order status (Baru → Diproses → Siap Diambil → Selesai), and view simple sales reports.

Success looks like: a customer can discover → customize → order → get a receipt in under 60 seconds on a phone; a barista can process and fulfil orders without leaving the dashboard.

## Brand Personality

Warm, unfussy, craft-forward. Voice in Indonesian. 3 words: **warm, simple, dependable**. The brand is a neighborhood coffee shop — familiar and comfortable, not corporate, not precious. Playful enough to feel human, restrained enough to stay legible.

## Anti-references

- Generic "AI SaaS" cream/beige body with dusty brown — the warmth lives in the brown + typography, not a tinted page.
- Over-decorated coffee "artisan" sites (dark moody stock, shaky handwriting fonts, grain overlays) — this is a transactional tool, not a brand campaign site.
- Crowded menu walls with no hierarchy; anything that makes a phone-order slow.
- Anthropomorphic/coffee-clipart illustration as decoration.

## Design Principles

1. **60 seconds to a receipt** — the customer path is the shortest possible chain: category → item → customize → checkout. Every extra tap is a defect.
2. **The brown does the warmth** — pure white surfaces; coffee brown + cream carry mood. No tinted page behind the content.
3. **Status is a language** — one consistent semantic color/shape vocabulary for order status across customer receipt and admin dashboard.
4. **Mobile-first, desktop-legible** — design for a thumb first; the admin table is dense but never cramped.
5. **Earned familiarity** — standard product affordances (cart sheet, steppers, segmented controls) that disappear into the task. No reinventing standard patterns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum on all interactive and body text (≥4.5:1; large text ≥3:1).
- Visible focus states on every interactive element.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected — crossfade or instant over slide/scale.
- Sensible defaults for size/sugar/ice (common choices pre-selected) to reduce decision cost.
- Color is never the only status signal — pair status colors with text labels and icons (color-blind safe).
